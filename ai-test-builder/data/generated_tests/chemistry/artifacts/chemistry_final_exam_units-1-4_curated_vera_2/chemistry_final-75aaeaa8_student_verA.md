## Part C: Problems and Calculations

*Section Plan: 4 questions | 20 marks*

*Instructions: Show complete work, include units, and use appropriate significant digits.*

26. 26. Aluminum reacts with oxygen according to 4Al(s) + 3O2(g) -> 2Al2O3(s). Calculate the mass of Al2O3 produced from 12.5 g of Al if oxygen is in excess. (5 marks)

27. 27. Calcium carbonate reacts with hydrochloric acid according to CaCO3(s) + 2HCl(aq) -> CaCl2(aq) + H2O(l) + CO2(g). If 10.0 g of CaCO3 reacts with 150.0 mL of 1.50 mol/L HCl, identify the limiting reagent and calculate the mass of CO2 produced. (5 marks)

28. 28. Silver nitrate reacts with sodium chloride according to AgNO3(aq) + NaCl(aq) -> AgCl(s) + NaNO3(aq). If 2.50 g of AgNO3 is used with excess NaCl and 1.85 g of AgCl is collected, determine the percent yield of AgCl. (5 marks)

29. 29. If 22.4 mL of NaOH(aq) is required to completely neutralize 25.0 mL of 0.200 mol/L H2SO4(aq), what is the concentration of the NaOH solution? Write the balanced chemical equation and show your work. (5 marks)
