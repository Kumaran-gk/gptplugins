## Composition and Reaction Stoichiometry

*Section Plan: 3 questions | 22 marks*

*Instructions: Show a complete stoichiometric setup. Write and balance any required chemical equation before solving.*

4. In excess oxygen, magnesium burns to form magnesium oxide. Calculate the amount in moles of MgO produced from 12.0 g of magnesium. (8 marks)

6. Aqueous sulfuric acid reacts with aqueous potassium hydroxide. If 3.25 g of H2SO4 reacts with excess KOH, calculate the mass of potassium sulfate produced. Write the balanced equation. Use appropriate notation in your response. (10 marks)
