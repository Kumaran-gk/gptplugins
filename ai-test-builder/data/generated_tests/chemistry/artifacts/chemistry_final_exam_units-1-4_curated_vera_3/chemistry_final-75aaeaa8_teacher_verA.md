31. [solubility_and_solvents | application | 6 marks] Rinsing with water dilutes the NaOH in the burette, so a larger volume is needed to reach the endpoint and the calculated acid concentration becomes too high if the standard NaOH concentration is still assumed. Overshooting the endpoint also makes the recorded NaOH volume too large, which again makes the calculated acid concentration too high. Water is suitable because its polarity dissolves ionic and polar substances well and allows the acid-base ions to interact in solution.
