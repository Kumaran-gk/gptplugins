## C. Lab Analysis (10 marks)

**21a (2 marks, Easy).** Δx = 19.2 - 0.0 = **19.2 m [E]**. Award 1 for endpoint subtraction and 1 for value/direction.

**21b (2 marks, Easy).** v_avg = Δx/Δt = 19.2/4.0 = **4.8 m/s [E]**. Award 1 for method and 1 for value/unit/direction.

**21c (2 marks, Medium).** Plot the five coordinates (0,0), (1,1.2), (2,4.8), (3,10.8), (4,19.2); label t (s) and x (m [E]); draw a smooth upward-curving trend. Award 1 for points/scale and 1 for labelled axes/curve.

**21d (2 marks, Medium).** Symmetric secant about 2.0 s: (x_3 - x_1)/(3.0 - 1.0) = (10.8 - 1.2)/2.0 = **4.8 m/s [E]**. For this quadratic data, the symmetric secant equals the instantaneous velocity at the midpoint. Award 1 for method and 1 for result/explanation.

**21e (2 marks, Medium).** At t = 2.0 s, 4.8 = ½a(2.0)², so **a = 2.4 m/s² [E]**. The increasing slope/curvature of the x-t graph shows non-uniform velocity. Award 1 for acceleration and 1 for graph interpretation.

## D. Applications (30 marks)

**22a (2 marks, Easy).** Total distance = 120 + 50 = **170 m**.

**22b (3 marks, Easy).** Δr = (120 E + 50 N) m. Magnitude = √(120² + 50²) = **130 m**; θ = tan⁻¹(50/120) = **22.6° N of E**. Award 1 for components/sketch, 1 for magnitude, 1 for direction.

**22c (2 marks, Easy).** Average speed = 170/40 = **4.25 m/s**. Award 1 for expression and 1 for result.

**22d (3 marks, Medium).** Average velocity = Δr/Δt = 130/40 = **3.25 m/s [22.6° N of E]**. Its magnitude is smaller than average speed because displacement (130 m) is shorter than distance (170 m). Award 1 for calculation, 1 for direction, 1 for explanation.

**23a (2 marks, Easy).** v_ix = 18.0 cos 30.0° = **15.6 m/s [E]**; v_iy = 18.0 sin 30.0° = **9.00 m/s [up]**. One mark each.

**23b (2 marks, Medium).** With launch height y_0 = 20.0 m, 0 = 20.0 + 9.00t - 4.90t². The positive root is **t = 3.14 s**. Award 1 for vertical equation and 1 for correct root.

**23c (2 marks, Medium).** Δx = v_ix t = (15.588...)(3.1376...) = **48.9 m [E]**. Award 1 for method and 1 for result.

**23d (4 marks, Hard).** v_fx = **15.6 m/s [E]**, v_fy = 9.00 - 9.80(3.1376...) = **-21.7 m/s**. Magnitude = √(15.588² + 21.749²) = **26.8 m/s**. Direction = tan⁻¹(21.749/15.588) = **54.4° below the eastward horizontal**. Award 1 for each component, 1 for magnitude, 1 for direction.

**24a (2 marks, Hard).** Vertical motion begins with v_iy = 0: 45.0 = ½(9.80)t², so **t = 3.03 s**. Award 1 for equation and 1 for time.

**24b (2 marks, Hard).** v_package/cart = v_package/ground - v_cart/ground = (20.0 E + 12.0 N) - (5.0 E) = **(15.0 E + 12.0 N) m/s**. Award 1 for frame relation and 1 for components.

**24c (3 marks, Hard).** In 3.03046 s, package displacement is (60.61 E + 36.37 N) m. Cart displacement is (15.15 E + 0 N) m. Thus the cart must start at **(45.5 m E, 36.4 m N)** relative to P. Award 1 for package position, 1 for cart motion, 1 for starting coordinates. Equivalent use of the relative velocity from part b earns full credit.

**24d (2 marks, Hard).** Initial separation = √(45.4569² + 36.3655²) = **58.2 m [38.7° N of E]**. Award 1 for magnitude and 1 for bearing.

**24e (1 mark, Hard).** Immediately after release, the package keeps the drone's horizontal ground velocity: **(20.0 E + 12.0 N) m/s**. Its horizontal velocity does not jump to zero because there is no horizontal impulse at release.
