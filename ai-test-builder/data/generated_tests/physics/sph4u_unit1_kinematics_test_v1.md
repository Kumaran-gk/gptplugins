## A. Multiple Choice (15 marks)

Choose the **one best answer**. Each question is worth 1 mark.

1. A student walks 30 m east and then 30 m west, returning to the start. The student's average velocity for the whole walk is:  
   A. 0 m/s  B. 30 m/s east  C. 60 m/s east  D. impossible to determine because time is missing

2. The slope of a position-time graph at one instant gives the object's:  
   A. displacement  B. instantaneous velocity  C. acceleration  D. distance travelled

3. The signed area between a velocity-time graph and the time axis gives:  
   A. acceleration  B. average speed  C. displacement  D. final position in every case

4. A speed of 72 km/h is equal to:  
   A. 2.0 m/s  B. 20 m/s  C. 72 m/s  D. 259 m/s

5. At the highest point of a projectile's flight, its acceleration is:  
   A. zero  B. horizontal  C. 9.80 m/s² downward  D. 9.80 m/s² upward

6. An object's velocity changes uniformly from +4.0 m/s to -2.0 m/s in 3.0 s. Its displacement during the interval is:  
   A. -3.0 m  B. 0 m  C. +3.0 m  D. +9.0 m

7. A displacement of 6.0 m east followed by 8.0 m north has a resultant of:  
   A. 10 m [53° N of E]  B. 10 m [37° N of E]  C. 14 m [53° N of E]  D. 2.0 m [N]

8. A puck's velocity changes from 4.0 m/s east to 3.0 m/s north. Its change in velocity is:  
   A. 5.0 m/s [37° N of W]  B. 5.0 m/s [37° N of E]  C. 7.0 m/s [N]  D. 1.0 m/s [W]

9. A ball is launched horizontally at 12 m/s and is airborne for 2.5 s. Its horizontal displacement is:  
   A. 4.8 m  B. 12 m  C. 30 m  D. 49 m

10. A boat moves 4.0 m/s north relative to the water while the current moves 3.0 m/s east. Its velocity relative to shore is:  
    A. 1.0 m/s [N]  B. 5.0 m/s [37° E of N]  C. 5.0 m/s [53° E of N]  D. 7.0 m/s [NE]

11. A ball is launched at 10.0 m/s, 30° above the horizontal, from a roof 12.0 m high. Its flight time to the ground is closest to:  
    A. 1.06 s  B. 1.56 s  C. 2.16 s  D. 3.06 s

12. A velocity-time graph has straight segments from (0 s, 0 m/s) to (2 s, +6 m/s), then to (4 s, -2 m/s). The **total distance** travelled is:  
    A. 10 m  B. 11 m  C. 12 m  D. 14 m

13. A pilot needs a ground velocity of 60 km/h north while the wind is 20 km/h east. The required air velocity is approximately:  
    A. 63 km/h [18° W of N]  B. 63 km/h [18° E of N]  C. 80 km/h [N]  D. 40 km/h [N]

14. A vehicle's velocity changes from 10 m/s north to 10 m/s east in 2.0 s. Its average acceleration is:  
    A. 0 m/s²  B. 5.0 m/s² [E]  C. 7.1 m/s² [45° S of E]  D. 10 m/s² [45° N of E]

15. A 4.0 m long car at 90 km/h overtakes a 200 m long train moving at 54 km/h in the same direction. From the instant the car's front reaches the train's rear until the car's rear clears the train's front, the time is:  
    A. 7.3 s  B. 20.4 s  C. 22.7 s  D. 73.4 s

## D. Applications (30 marks)

22. A search robot travels 120 m east, then 50 m north, in a total time of 40 s. Use a labelled vector sketch or components.  
    a. Find its total distance travelled. **(2)**  
    b. Find its resultant displacement, including direction. **(3)**  
    c. Find its average speed. **(2)**  
    d. Find its average velocity, including direction. Explain briefly why the speed and velocity magnitudes differ. **(3)**

23. A rescue flare leaves a 20.0 m high platform at 18.0 m/s, 30.0° above the horizontal. Take east as +x and up as +y.  
    a. Determine its initial horizontal and vertical velocity components. **(2)**  
    b. Determine its flight time before it reaches level ground. **(2)**  
    c. Determine its horizontal displacement. **(2)**  
    d. Determine its velocity just before impact, as a magnitude and direction. **(4)**

A cart on the road moves at 5.0 m/s east. Find where the cart must be **at the release instant**, relative to P, for the package to land in it. Take east as +x and north as +y.  
    a. Determine the package's flight time. **(2)**  
    b. Determine the package's horizontal velocity relative to the cart as components. **(2)**  
    c. Determine the required initial east and north coordinates of the cart relative to P. **(3)**  
    d. Express that initial position as a distance and bearing from P. **(2)**  
    e. State the package's horizontal velocity relative to the ground immediately after release and explain why. **(1)**
