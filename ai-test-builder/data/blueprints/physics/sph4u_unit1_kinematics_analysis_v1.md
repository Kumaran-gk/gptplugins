# SPH4U Unit 1 Kinematics: source analysis and generation notes

## Sources used

- The one-page September 2026 syllabus sets the coverage and identifies Test #1 as kinematics. It lists motion graphs, non-uniform-motion lab work, two-dimensional displacement and velocity change, projectiles, and relative motion.
- Nelson Physics 12 Chapter 1 supplies definitions, graph interpretations, equations, and worked styles for those topics. The supplied PDF's printed page and lesson numbering do not fully match the syllabus headings, so named topics and assigned page content determined coverage.
- `TrainingQuestions1.jpeg` shows difficult multipart projectile and relative-motion word problems. `TrainingQuestions2.jpeg` shows graphical vector operations and change in velocity under uniform acceleration. Neither image establishes a full test-paper section layout.

## Observed versus inferred pattern

**Observed:** The user specified 15 multiple-choice items, 5 true/false items, one 10-mark lab item, and three 10-mark applications. The training sheets call for vector diagrams, direction conventions, several calculation steps, and real-motion contexts.

**Inferred:** Tricky distractors should target direction, signed graph area, speed versus velocity, projectile component independence, and the relative-motion frame. The lab item should use a data table and ask for graph interpretation and an instantaneous-velocity estimate.

**Uncertainty:** The two training sheets are not a complete prior test, and no original marking rubric or official time limit was supplied. This version assumes one mark for each selected-response item, for 60 marks total. The difficulty target is implemented by marks: 20 normal, 20 medium hard, and 20 hard (33.3% each). The teacher key tags every mark for audit.
