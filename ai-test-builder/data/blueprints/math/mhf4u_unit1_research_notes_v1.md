# MHF4U Unit 1 test pattern: research notes

## Observed evidence

1. The supplied Unit-1 IndexSheet names functions, domain/range, interval notation, absolute value, graph properties, transformations, inverses, piecewise functions, and average and instantaneous rates of change. It schedules a task, review, and test after 12 instruction classes.
2. Ontario's [Grades 11 and 12 mathematics curriculum](https://www.edu.gov.on.ca/eng/curriculum/secondary/math1112currb.pdf) identifies four assessment categories: Knowledge and Understanding, Thinking, Communication, and Application (curriculum pp. 24-25). MHF4U includes interpreting average rates from multiple representations, approximating instantaneous rates with shrinking intervals, and linking secant and tangent slopes (curriculum pp. 95-96).
3. An [unofficial public preview of a MHF4U Unit 1 test](https://www.coursehero.com/file/86519856/Test-1pdf/) lists almost the same Unit 1 topics and labels questions using K/U, T/I, C, and A marks. Its visible questions include piecewise graphing, transformations, inverses, and rate of change. The upload is a pattern clue, not a verified board standard; no text or numbers were reused.
4. A [Toronto District School Board school course outline](https://schoolweb.tdsb.on.ca/Portals/foresthill/docs/MHF4U1%20Course%20Outline.pdf) shows category weights of 30% Knowledge/Understanding, 30% Application, 20% Communication, and 20% Thinking, and calls a different set of lessons "Unit 1." This supports treating unit-test details as locally determined. The direct PDF link was unavailable during verification; its indexed search excerpt supplied these details.

## Inferred practice pattern

- A 60-mark, 90-minute paper with 12 original constructed-response questions.
- Four sections, 15 marks each, to make the four achievement categories visible. Equal weighting is a chosen practice format and is not required province-wide.
- Domain/range and graph features first; multi-step transformations, inverses, and piecewise work next; explanations and contextual rate problems last.
- Graphs must show endpoints and labels. Context answers need units and interpretation.
- The teacher's actual test may differ in timing, category weights, calculator rules, and question count. This test prepares the skills in the supplied index rather than predicting a specific teacher's paper.

## Scope decision

The research guides assessment format, but the supplied index controls content. Ontario/OAME materials that call polynomial functions "Unit 1" use a different sequencing and were not substituted for this student's syllabus.
